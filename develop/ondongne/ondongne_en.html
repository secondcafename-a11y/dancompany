FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the other person doesn't show up?
On the appointment card, you can report a "No-Show" within 48 hours after the scheduled time. Repeated offenses will lower their Manner Temperature and restrict account access. If plans change, anyone can cancel with a single tap in 3 seconds — fostering respectful cancellations over ghosting.
What items are strictly prohibited from sharing?
Opened food, medications, used cosmetics, critical safety gear (e.g., used child car seats, helmets), alcohol, tobacco, live pets, and bulk items intended for resale are prohibited. The post creation screen provides full guidelines, and non-compliant listings are reviewed with clear feedback.
How long should I wait if a lister hasn't replied to my request?
The request screen displays the lister’s response deadline. If they do not respond within that timeframe, the turn automatically passes to the next requester, so you don't need to chase them up.
Will other users be able to see my exact home address?
No. Public listings only display the neighborhood district name. Precise address details are shared exclusively with the confirmed recipient inside the secure appointment card, even for doorstep drop-offs.
How does Manner Temperature increase or decrease?
It goes up with completed shares and warm tag reviews (e.g., "Very Punctual", "Friendly"), and drops when verified reports accumulate. Malicious or unfair ratings are excluded after moderation, and 3 consecutive safe shares will reset warning indicators. You can view your history on your profile.
When and how much am I charged for using the app?
Nothing is charged when you sign up. Your first month is a completely free trial, and we notify you 2 days prior to expiration. If you share 3 items a month, the following month is automatically 100% free. You only purchase a 30-day Warmth Pass if you share less in a given month.
How do I deal with rude or unpleasant users?
You can immediately report and block users via the top-right corner of the chat screen. Blocked users cannot message you or view your posts, and they will not be notified of the block. Reports receive an instant confirmation receipt and are audited within 24 hours.
