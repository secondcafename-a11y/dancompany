HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from posting to done, with the vague parts removed

Wherever neighbourly giving actually breaks down — arranging a time, no-shows, confirming it happened — we built in a safeguard.

1

Photograph and post

Up to five photos, pick a category, tap “Write with AI” and you're done. You can flag doorstep handover as your preference.

Before you post, the giving rules and prohibited items appear on one screen

2

Request it in chat

When several neighbours apply, the giver picks one and confirms the booking. Everyone else is notified automatically that it's closed.

A reply deadline is shown, so nobody is left waiting on silence

3

The confirmation card

Time, place and handover method are pinned to a card, and the receiver is issued a PIN. Add-to-calendar and cancel sit on the same screen.

If plans change, cancelling takes three seconds — cancelling beats going quiet

4

PIN and review

Enter the PIN after the handover to close it. Tag reviews like “always on time” raise your manner temperature and earn a stamp.

Just tap the tags — a review takes ten seconds

Where every neighbour starts

How manner temperature works — completed handovers and warm tag reviews raise it; accumulated reports lower it. Fall too far and participation can be limited; three safe handovers in a row clear the warning. Unfair ratings are reviewed and excluded.
